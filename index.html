FAQ

A single token on PulseChain that represents a basket of core ecosystem assets — PLSX, INC, HEX, eHEX, WBTC, and WETH. Deposit PLS, get exposure to all of them in one token, instead of managing six separate positions.
Shares are minted based on the fund's current NAV (net asset value) — the actual value of the basket backing existing holders — rather than a fixed ratio to PLS. This keeps new deposits fair to existing holders regardless of how the basket has moved since launch.
A small fee on deposits and redemptions (shown live in the Index Stats section above) funds a PLS rewards pool distributed to PINDEX holders. There's no ongoing management fee beyond that.
